Advanced Sail Trim Masterclass

A focused program for sailors who already race multihulls and want to close the gap between good and great — the specific trim habits that separate solid club racers from championship-level crews.

What It Is

The Advanced Sail Trim Masterclass isn't an introduction to multihull sailing — it's a deep, coached focus on the details that actually move the needle once the fundamentals are already solid: reading pressure before it hits, tuning and marking settings before a start, and building the kind of trim habits that hold up under race pressure instead of falling apart the moment things get tactical.

Who It's For

This program assumes real multihull racing experience already. It's built for sailors who can comfortably handle the boat and want to sharpen specific technique, not sailors looking for a first introduction — if that's what you need, the 4-Day Intensive is the better starting point.

What You'll Work On

Expect a direct focus on the habits that distinguish championship-level trim: continuous, proactive adjustment instead of setting sails once and leaving them; two-boat tuning before a start, with lines marked so a fast setting can be found again instantly; reading sea state and chop as deliberately as wind; and getting specific, real-time feedback from a coach who can see what you can't feel from the boat.

Format

A shorter, more concentrated format than the 4-Day Intensive, built around focused coaching time rather than a ground-up progression. Best timed when you already have upcoming racing on the calendar you want to be sharper for.

If You're Not Sure This Is Right

If you're new to multihull sailing, start with the 4-Day Intensive instead — this program assumes you can already handle the boat. If you're comfortable on the water but have a specific regatta bearing down on you, Training Clinic & Race Prep is more targeted to that timeline.
