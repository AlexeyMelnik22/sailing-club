About

SMSC is a non-profit association specializing in nautical activities, especially multihull sailing. SMSC was founded with a vision of creating opportunities for sailors to learn about high performance sailing and to experience our island's magnificent winds and turquoise waters.

Join the Fleet

You do not need to be a seasoned racer to find a home at SMSC. Whether you are curious about multihulls, a monohull sailor or someone looking to escape the snow, we provide the platform, the knowledge, and a welcoming community to get you out on the water.
