The trim and weight-placement habits on a multihull are genuinely different from what you're used to, which is exactly the point: our programs are built to get you past that learning curve quickly with a coach on hand, not to leave you to figure it out alone.

Yes. Most of our training programs put you into a boat with a coach and other sailors, so you don't need to bring your own crew. It's a common way for solo travelers to get real time on the water and meet other sailors on the island.
