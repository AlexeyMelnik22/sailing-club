Multihull Sailing in Caribbean Breeze

Welcome to Saint Martin Sailing Club (SMSC). Located in the heart of the Caribbean trade winds, SMSC is dedicated to high-performance multihull sailing. Our focus is squarely on providing opportunities to learn about and master the technical mechanics of the modern multihull platforms.

Whether you are here to experience flying a hull for the first time, to refine your wing mast rotation, secure a charter for a regatta, or line up for weekend fleet racing, SMSC provides the infrastructure, the boats, and the knowledge to push your limits.
